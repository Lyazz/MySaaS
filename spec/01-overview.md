# Overview

## Product
Multi-tenant SaaS e-commerce for Algeria focused on higher conversion for COD.

## Target
Medium retailers.

## Core goals
- Speed / modern UX (mobile-first)
- More orders via COD confirmation
- SEO storefront per tenant
- Scalable for 100 tenants initially

## MVP scope
- Tenant storefront:
  - Home, category, product, static pages (About/Contact)
- Admin:
  - Products + variants + categories
  - Orders management
  - Theme: logo, colors, homepage sections
- Checkout:
  - No account required
  - Phone required
  - COD ordering
- Super-admin:
  - Suspend/delete tenants
  - Revenue overview
  - Plan limits
  - Impersonation

## Not in MVP (planned)
- Chargily payment
- Delivery integrations (start after MVP)
- Advanced marketing automation
